# Recording the intro video

The game opens with an intro made live from the game's own scenes. If you record a real gameplay video,
save it as `public/intro.mp4` (H.264, 1920×1080, under about 50 MB) and the game will play it instead.

## How to record (Windows)

1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge, press **⤢ Full screen**, and choose **High graphics**.
2. Press **Win + G** (Xbox Game Bar) and click record, or use OBS Studio with a Display Capture source.
3. Record the shots below (about 60–75 seconds in total), then trim with the Windows Photos/Clipchamp editor.
4. Add subtitles or a voice-over with the script, export as MP4, and save it as `public/intro.mp4`.

## Shot list and script

| # | What to show in the game | Say / subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assembly Hall, slow walk around the spacecraft | “MissionForge: design a real space mission, from the first idea to the last byte of data.” |
| 2 | Mission Control briefing screen | “Every mission starts with a science question.” |
| 3 | Imaging Lab: open the picker, flip between HiRISE-class and CTX-class cards | “Choose real NASA instruments. Each card shows the real mass and power, the price, and the trade-offs.” |
| 4 | Install a heavy camera and watch the Live budget bars jump | “Every component you select makes a difference: more power, more mass, more fuel, more money.” |
| 5 | Power Room and Communications Room choices | “Give it power, a way to talk to Earth, and protection.” |
| 6 | Launch Vehicle Hall: the Recommended rocket | “Pick the cheapest rocket that still leaves a 25% margin.” |
| 7 | Fueling, then the Go/No-Go console | “Load just enough propellant, and let the four roles review the design.” |
| 8 | Launch: staging, then the flight tour past the planets | “Launch, and learn what lies along the way.” |
| 9 | Debrief with the cause trace | “If something fails, the debrief shows exactly which decision caused it. Now it is your turn.” |

Remember the event rules: do not show anyone under 18 in the video, and credit NASA for any NASA images.
